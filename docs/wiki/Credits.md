# Credits

## MS Icons

All resource icons in AIS BluePrinter come from **[MS Icons](https://msicons.com/)**, a
community project by **Daniel Bradley** ([github.com/DanielBradley1/msicons](https://github.com/DanielBradley1/msicons))
that makes the official Microsoft Azure architecture icons easy to browse and use. Thank you!

The icons themselves are the **Microsoft Azure architecture icons, © Microsoft**, and are subject
to Microsoft's [icon terms of use](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/architecture/icons/). They are
not part of this repository: they are loaded from msicons.com at runtime, or from a mirror you
create yourself with `tools/mirror-icons.js`.
